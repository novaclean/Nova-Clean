Training mats and gym floors work hard. Sweat, bare feet, shoes carried in from outside and constant foot traffic leave dirt, residue and odours that everyday sweeping and mopping can’t fully remove.

Professional cleaning with the right equipment helps keep your training space cleaner and more presentable for members, and helps protect the flooring you have invested in.

We use specialised mat-cleaning equipment, like the machine in the photos on this page, and we can schedule work outside your class times, including after hours. Cleaning can be one-off or part of a recurring maintenance program.
